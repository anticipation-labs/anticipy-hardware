- **Raytac AN54LV-15 is real and buyable in EVT quantity.** Raytac lists the 6.4 x 8.4 x 1.5 mm module as an active product. DigiKey Marketplace shows 45 pieces at USD 4.30, but Raytac—not DigiKey—ships them in approximately 14 days and charges USD 25 flat freight. Raytac's officially linked eBay listing adds five pieces at USD 6.00 plus USD 5.00 worldwide shipping.

- **The battery certification evidence is not purchase-release evidence for the custom NTC pack.** The standard page is tagged "IEC62133 Certified" and the drawing says the battery *parts* are IEC 62133 and RoHS compliant; another supplier page markets an "IEC62133 Certificate" version with different 50 mm wires and 26 mm length. No certificate/test-report number, exact-pack CB report, or exact LP571225 UN 38.3 test summary is downloadable from those pages. Obtain and match documents to the signed NTC pack drawing.
- **A complete exact-candidate cart is not executable today.** DigiKey currently shows zero VC0720B015F motors, while Vybronics' page still says 651 at DigiKey. That conflict must be resolved in writing. The custom battery is RFQ-only.

## LP571225 release requirements

The production item should remain `BAT-ANT-200-001`, not merely `LP571225`. Require a supplier-signed drawing that fixes all of the following:

- finished maximum envelope **27.0 x 12.5 x 6.0 mm**, including PCM, tape, weld tabs, NTC and lead exit; the public comparator drawing permits 26 ± 1 mm finished length, so 26 mm cannot be treated as the maximum;
- 3.7 V nominal, 200 mAh minimum/accepted capacity tolerance, 1S1P PCM thresholds, permitted charge current and temperature limits;
- **three leads**, exact colors/polarity/length/connector or stripped termination;
- NTC **10 kOhm ±1% at 25 C, B3380** and its physical placement on the cell;
- swelling allowance and lifecycle acceptance criteria;
- the exact finished pack's **IEC 62133-2 test report or CB certificate**, **UN 38.3 test summary**, SDS/MSDS, RoHS/REACH declarations, and dangerous-goods shipping declaration.

The supplier page's incorrect “27.75 Wh” field (the same page otherwise says 0.74 Wh) is an additional reason to rely on a signed drawing rather than web copy. Adding an NTC may also consume the entire 6.0 mm thickness limit; approve measured samples before enclosure release.

## Delivery, tax and schedule conclusion

- [DigiKey Canada](https://www.digikey.ca/en/help-support/delivery-information/delivery-time-and-cost) says regular CAD orders are DDP, free-delivery above CAD 100, and usually reach Canada in about 24 hours after dispatch, with customs capable of adding delay. Marketplace material is separate, CPT, excluded from free freight, and has duty/customs/tax due on delivery.
- Mouser Canada presents CAD orders as DDP and says prices include duty/customs on select shipping methods; GST/PST remain applicable. The switch-only order is below its CAD 100 free-freight threshold.
- The Raytac eBay, LCSC and battery-supplier routes are cross-border shipments. Budget checkout freight, brokerage, GST and BC PST as applicable; do not apply the US-only 25% Vybronics tariff note to a Canadian landed-cost model. Lithium batteries may also attract dangerous-goods freight and carrier restrictions.

**Reject a two-week promise.** A 2026-09-12 arrival for exact, assembled EVT units is not credible: the DigiKey Marketplace AN54LV-15 alone takes approximately 14 days merely to dispatch; its official-linked eBay page showed a Sep 4–22 range for a generic displayed location rather than West Vancouver; the exact motor has no verified distributor stock; and the required NTC battery is a custom RFQ item with no published sample lead.

For planning only, late September/October is the first defensible **3-EVT** window *after* written motor allocation and custom-battery sample confirmation, plus PCB assembly and transit. It is not presently a commit date. The **50-unit** build is not schedulable today: obtain a direct Raytac allocation with spares and a written 50-pack exception/lead from LiPol first. If the battery requires new production, its published 6–7 week clock precedes assembly and shipping, making November 2026 an aggressive planning floor rather than a promise.
