# 04 — Mechanical

**Print-ready is not the same as production-ready.** Nothing here has been validated on a sealed,
assembled unit.

## Two parallel enclosure lineages exist

This is the mechanical equivalent of the processor contradiction, and it is easy to miss because
the two live in different folders with different naming:

| Lineage | Form factor | Source | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ordered-shell recovery v0.2** | 51 × 22 × 14.5 mm body + 4.50 mm midband, coin motor, M2 screw retention | Generated by a Python recovery script | `../02_PROTOTYPE_XIAO/CAD/`, `PRINT_READY/xiao_prototype_plates/` |
| **`anticipy-shell` v5.1** | 26.5 × 61.2 × 12.6 mm flat pill tag (33.4 × 80.7 × 16.2 mm in the v5 storage build), click tongue-and-groove joint, microSD pocket | Parametric **OpenSCAD** | `OPENSCAD_SHELL_PROGRAM_v5.1/` |
| **Production CAD** | 50.500 × 20.680 × 10.800 mm envelope, 16 solids | Execution v1.0 | `REFERENCE_STEP/` |

These are **three different products**, not three views of one. Nobody has written down which is
current. The R1 brief adds a fourth number: 56.0 × 23.0 × 12.0 mm.

## Layout

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `OPENSCAD_SHELL_PROGRAM_v5.1/` | The only *parametric* enclosure source on the machine. `pendant.scad` with a documented v1 → v5.1 history, 12 STL exports, print plates, a clearance coupon, a Bambu print recipe, a dimensioned drawing, an XIAO+microSD pinmap, a power/storage budget, a per-unit BOM with real sources and prices, an 8-step assembly sequence, and a 100-unit MJF-vs-CNC cost comparison. |
| `MACHINIST_DRAWINGS/` | **The only machinist-facing 2D drawing pair found anywhere** — `production_aluminum_cap_drawing.pdf` + `_outline.dxf`. If a CNC vendor is ever engaged, this is what they get. |
| `REFERENCE_STEP/` | Execution v1.0 production CAD. 23/23 mechanical checks passed — **against the 50.5 × 20.68 × 10.8 envelope only**. |
| `PRINT_READY/` | Plates and parts believed printable. Includes the XIAO prototype plates. |
| `SUPERSEDED_AND_MOCKUPS/` | Older meshes, mockups and visual studies. Kept for history. |

## Known unresolved mechanical issues

- **The v5 joint does not lock.** The 2026-08-18 P2S print failed on joint clearance — recorded in
  the OpenSCAD program's own build book as "zero tolerance, doesn't even try to lock". Whether a
  corrected plate was ever printed successfully is not recorded.
- **Material and adhesive conflict.** `README` in the OpenSCAD program specifies Bambu PLA Metal
  with gel CA glue; its own `BUILD_BOOK`, `print-plan` and `build-steps` specify black ABS. These
  have different shrinkage and different bonding requirements.
- **A permanently glued shell conflicts with DFU recovery.** The shell is designed to be glued
  shut, but firmware recovery depends on 1200-baud USB touch and BLE DFU being reachable. If the
  radio or the crystal fails inside a sealed unit, there is no documented way back in.

## Oversized files

`SUPERSEDED_AND_MOCKUPS/OVERSIZED_MESHES_500mah_2026-08-22/` holds two STLs of 68.8 MB and
64.8 MB. Both exceed GitHub's 50 MB warning threshold (neither hits the 100 MB block). They are
superseded and kept only for history — the first candidates to drop if repository size matters.
