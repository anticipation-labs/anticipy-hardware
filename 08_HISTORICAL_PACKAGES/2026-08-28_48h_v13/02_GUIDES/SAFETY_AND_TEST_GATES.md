# Safety and test gates

This package is for one engineering prototype.  It is not authorization to
ship a customer product.

- Use only the controlled protected BBM 602535 cell or update the CAD for a
  different exact battery.
- Verify battery polarity with a multimeter before connection.
- Never solder, program, connect to a wall-powered computer or charge while
  the pendant is worn.
- Never charge unattended.
- Stop using the battery if it swells, becomes hot, smells unusual, is dented
  or is punctured.
- Do not seal the first unit until audio, Bluetooth, storage, button, haptic,
  charge and 16-hour runtime tests pass.
- PLA Silk+ is a prototype material.  It is not drop-certified, waterproof,
  heat-safe in a hot car or suitable for customer production.
- Wear-test with a breakaway necklace connection.

Physical release gates:

1. 20 hours of audio can be written and read back without corrupt files.
2. The backlog resumes after iPhone reconnection without losing sequence.
3. A fully charged sealed unit records for at least 16 hours at room temperature.
4. Surface temperature stays acceptable during recording and charging.
5. Ten 1 m drops onto plywood produce no exposed battery, loose part or sharp edge.
6. A full-day walk test produces no rattle, false button presses or wire failure.
