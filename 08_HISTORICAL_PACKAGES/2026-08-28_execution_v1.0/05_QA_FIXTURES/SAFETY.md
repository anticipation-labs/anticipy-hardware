# Safety rules

These fixtures include moving parts, a 1.2 m fall, springs and a lithium-powered product. An experienced adult must supervise construction and every live-device run.

## Drop stand

- Anchor both 2020 masts to a weighted base **and** a wall or rigid bench.
- Enclose the complete fall and rebound zone with clear polycarbonate.
- The target centre must have an unobstructed 200 x 200 mm corridor.
- Keep hands outside the guard. Use remote ARM and momentary DROP controls.
- Wear eye protection.
- Run 30 battery-free dummy releases before a live unit.
- Inspect the target after each 26-drop sequence; replace it if dented, cracked, wet or warped.
- A guide carriage falling with the product is prohibited. It changes the impact energy and can eject parts.

## Rattle cradle

- Guard the rotor, belt and pulleys.
- Wire the guard switch normally closed so a broken wire stops motion.
- Current-limit the stepper and use a belt that can slip before crushing the cassette.
- Never tighten the cassette beyond its hard plastic stop.
- Reverse at +/-170 degrees. Continuous rotation can wind and break a sensor cable.

## Battery handling

- Do not put a swollen, dented, hot, leaking, creased or previously severe-drop-damaged pouch cell in either fixture.
- Do not recharge a dropped test unit until an adult-qualified person has inspected it under the product’s written battery procedure.
- Quarantine suspect units in a noncombustible, ventilated location away from people and combustibles.
- Never short, crush, puncture, bend, heat, overcharge or deliberately ignite a cell locally.
- Run powered spray or mechanical tests on internal battery only, never while USB or a wall-powered programmer is attached.

The supplied dummy is intentionally inert. Add steel washers to match production mass; never use a loose cell as ballast.
