Anticipy Build Book

Three wearable, working pendant pre-units, printed on the P2S, that stream audio, capture offline for 4–8 hours, reconnect, snap onto a charger, and hang on a chain your hands can actually operate. Every number in here was researched or machine-verified today. Nothing is decorative.

02 Print tonight

Step 0 — the 10-minute coupon (added Aug 19, after the Devin-night print). Print COUPON_FIRST.3mf first, same ABS filament and same profile as the plate below. It is four thumb-size slices of the real click joint: one tongue piece and three groove pieces at 0.16 / 0.22 / 0.28 mm clearance (count the little edge notches: 1 = 0.16, 2 = 0.22, 3 = 0.28). Press the tongue piece into each groove piece. The snuggest one that still clicks fully home by hand is this printer's number. If it's 0.16 (1 notch) — print the plate below unchanged. If it's 0.22 or 0.28, regenerate the six halves first with -D LIP_CLR_OVERRIDE=0.22 (or 0.28) and rebuild the plate — one command per half, ~5 minutes total. This is what makes "the halves will click" a measured fact instead of a hope.

Open ~/Desktop/anticipy-shell/PRINT_ME_v5_3pendants.3mf in Bambu Studio. It holds six halves = three pendants: V1-STORAGE (the unit), V2-STORAGE-ROOMY (fit insurance), V1-standard (thin control, 12.6 mm).

  • Filament: your black ABS — select the ABS filament profile so the chamber flaps stay closed. AMS not needed (single material).
  • Plate: Textured PEI, 90–100 °C bed, thin glue-stick film. Nozzle 265 °C.
  • Process: 0.12 mm layers, 4 walls, 25–40% gyroid, Arachne on, seam Back/Aligned, elephant-foot compensation 0.15 mm, 5 mm brim, part fan ≤30%, aux fan OFF, door + lid closed.
  • Halves lie exactly as arranged — flat face down, no supports, don't rotate anything.
  • ABS makes styrene fumes: ventilated room, carbon filter on.
Why not the silver silk PLA you bought for this: its chrome sheen is destroyed by the sanding the seam needs, it strings on fine text, and PLA softens at 55 °C — a parked car kills a LiPo-carrying PLA pendant. The black ABS + paint stack in §07 looks more like the website's titanium, not less.
Done when the coupon has named a clearance, six halves (at that clearance) are off the plate with clean USB openings (one pass of a needle file), and the click joints snap shut dry.

03 Wiring — the diagram

  • Solder-near-LiPo SOP: kapton over the pouch, never rest the iron near it, heat-shrink every joint, and before every power-up: continuity-check 3V3↔GND and 5V↔GND for shorts.
  • Bench first: wire the SPARE XIAO + SD module on loose leads and prove firmware BEFORE touching the good soldered unit.
  • D7 stays free. The old assumption that CS lives on D7 is wrong — stock firmware uses D0.
Done when the bench mule mounts the card and audio/a01.txt grows with BLE off.

04 Battery map

  • Cell: BLL 752042 · 3.7 V · 500 mAh · 1.85 Wh · max 8.0×20.5×43 mm; the shell bay allows 10% swelling headroom.
  • Prototype accepts ~0.5–1 mA SD idle (VCC tied to 3V3). Production re-adds the power-gate on D1 + P-MOSFET.
  • Charge at 16 kbps offline if configurable: halves both the storage AND the hour-long worst-case BLE sync drain.
  • LiPo rules: mic hole stays open (it's the vent), no charging in a hot car or direct sun, first full charge attended.

05 Firmware — port, don't write

The offline system already exists in BasedHardware/omi → firmware/devkit, targeting this exact board: when BLE drops, Opus packets append to audio/aXX.txt on the SD card; a GATT storage service streams files back with offset-resume, then deletes on ack. The Omi app has the sync screen. Your slim dual-hatch build has this deliberately disabled (CONFIG_SPI=n, CONFIG_FAT_FILESYSTEM_ELM=n).

  1. Patch 1 (mandatory): the devkit gates SD power via P0.19, which the XIAO doesn't break out. Stub the enable-GPIO in sdcard.c and tie module VCC to 3V3.
  2. Patch 2 (mandatory): drive P0.13 as gpio output-low at boot → 100 mA charge. Skipping it silently doubles charge time.
  3. Check 3: confirm the Opus bitrate is configurable and set 16 kbps for offline capture; if not, 32 kbps still fits the card easily.
  4. Build for the XIAO Sense target, flash the BENCH mule (1200-baud touch or double-tap RST — both proven on your hardware), verify mount + file growth offline, then the full sync loop against the app.
  5. App decision by Aug 25: if the Anticipy iOS app doesn't speak the storage service (302957…5781/5782), demo offline sync with the stock Omi app and port the client next sprint (+2–3 days).
  6. Torture pass: yank power mid-write (FATFS batching bounds the loss — verify), and >2 disconnect cycles so file rollover + app drain both prove out.
Done when the mule survives: record 30 min offline → reconnect → full sync → audio decodes → file deleted.

06 Assemble — test before you glue

The v5 shell CLICK-CLOSES and holds. That means the complete unit gets fully tested while still openable. Nothing is welded until it has already proven itself. That is the zero-failure mechanism.

  1. Clip the header pins flush (glasses on — pins fly). Kapton over the stubs and the battery pouch.
  2. Line both bays with 1–2 mm adhesive EVA. Battery into the back-half bay on foam, wire-end toward the board. Wires must NEVER cross in the closing channel — fold the upper-pad wire with a 2 mm sideways jog, kapton the fold.
  3. SD module lies flat on the battery's face in its pocket (storage halves have the depth for it), caps across its power pins, leads dressed through the tunnel.
  4. Board into the front half nose-first at ~15° tilt, USB nose leading into the port hole, then flat onto the shelf. Sanity check: RST sits toward the bail, LEDs visible — the amber glow through the front dot is your product LED, exactly like the website's.
  5. Seat the magnetic USB-C tip into the flush port from outside.
  6. Click shut. Run the whole gate (§09) now, unglued. Anything fails → pop it open, fix, repeat.
  7. Only after a full pass: open, thin acetone coat on both rims (or CA gel), close, rubber-band, wiggle a waxed jig cable in the port at 2 min so nothing bonds to it. Cure 24 h. Fill the seam with acetone+ABS slurry.
Done when a welded unit re-passes the gate after 24 h cure.

07 The titanium finish

  1. Sand the seam 220 → 320 dry, then 400 → 600 wet, over the whole body. Mask the mic dot (toothpick) and the port (tape) through every step from here.
  2. Primer ×2, wet-sand 400 between coats.
  3. Metal: Rub 'n Buff Silver Leaf cut with a touch of Ebony, rubbed and buffed. (Backup: Rust-Oleum Metallic Aluminum, 2 light coats.)
  4. Grain: 0000 steel wool, straight single-direction strokes along the long axis only. This is the stroke that reads "machined".
  5. Seal: Krylon SATIN clear ×2 — mandatory on skin. Cure 48 h before anyone wears it.
  6. No acetone vapor, no heat — both would erase the debossed wordmark.
Done when a stranger at the shop asks where you bought it.

08 Chain + the two experiments

  • Every demo unit: 22" cut from the curb roll, threaded straight through the Ø6.4 bail (5 mm curb passes; no jump ring needed), AIEX magnetic converter closing it at the nape. On, off, one hand, eyes closed.
  • Experiment A — magnet-snap bail (2 shells): Ø9.2×3.4 mm glue-in pockets, one 9×3 disc in the pendant top, mate disc in a tiny printed barrel on the chain end. Sharpie polarity on the stack BEFORE separating discs. Layout keeps the magnet >10 mm from the BLE antenna — verified in CAD. Bench-test pull with a luggage scale; expect ~1 kg+ axial. Snag = breakaway (feature and risk).
  • Experiment B — embedded pogo puck (1 shell): when the pucks arrive Aug 19–20, measure with calipers and I'll cut the parametric pocket + reprint that back half same-day. Diode rules per §03.

09 The gate — what "working pre-unit" means

One unit passes when ALL of these are true, in one continuous session, on battery:

  • ☐ Click-assembled (or welded) with padding — nothing rattles when shaken next to your ear.
  • ☐ Advertises, pairs, streams live audio to the app; amber dot glows through the front.
  • ☐ Phone leaves for 30+ minutes → pendant keeps capturing to SD.
  • ☐ Phone returns → sync completes untouched; the captured audio plays and is intelligible.
  • ☐ Snap-charges on its dock; charge current confirmed ≥90 mA (USB meter).
  • ☐ Worn for a full day on the chain: clasp operable one-handed, no skin complaints, no edges.
  • ☐ Survives the drop test: chest height onto wood, three orientations, still passes the above.
Truth in demos Pre-units are painted ABS at ~40 g, not 8 g titanium; charging is magnetic contact, not 15-ft over-the-air; "3-day battery" = mixed use, not 72 h streaming. Say so to anyone who handles one — the site's claims stay roadmap, the pre-unit proves the system. (Qi coils physically don't fit this shell width; true over-the-air charging is B2B-only tech in 2026.)
