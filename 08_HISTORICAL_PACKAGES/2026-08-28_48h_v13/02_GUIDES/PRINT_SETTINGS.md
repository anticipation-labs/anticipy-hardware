# Print this on the Bambu Lab P2S

Open `../01_PRINT/PRINT_THIS_ONE_UNIT_P2S.3mf`.

Use these settings for the PLA Silk+ you already own:

- Printer: Bambu Lab P2S, 0.4 mm nozzle
- Material preset: your exact PLA Silk+ brand; dry filament first if it snaps
- Layer height: 0.20 mm
- Wall generator: Arachne
- Wall loops: 5
- Top shell layers: 6
- Bottom shell layers: 6
- Sparse infill: 45% gyroid
- Outer-wall speed: 40 mm/s
- Brim: 5 mm on the base and lid
- Supports: off
- Print orientation: do not rotate the supplied objects
- Plate: clean textured PEI; use the glue specified by Bambu for your plate if required

## If the previous print broke

The old file had thin 1 mm walls.  This one has 1.6 mm walls, thicker rails, a
loop printed flat in the strongest direction, and screw closure instead of
fragile snaps.

Let the plate cool fully before removal.  Flex the plate; do not pry the loop.
If the silk filament itself snaps while feeding or leaves gaps, dry it before
printing the final shell.
