# How the finished pendant does not rattle

## Like you are two

The shell is an **egg carton**. Every little part gets its own little bed. A
soft pad takes up tiny gaps, and the lid squeezes everything gently so nothing
can jump around.

## What holds each thing

| Thing | Its seat | Its backup restraint |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | molded cradle with end stops | thin qualified PORON pad; no hard point pressing the pouch |
| PCB | perimeter ledge and locating posts | controlled snaps/screws; never loose wires |
| Haptic motor | round molded pocket | approved adhesive plus keeper; wires receive strain relief |
| Button | keyed plunger guide | silicone boot controls preload and seals the side opening |
| Microphones | direct sound ducts | compressed acoustic gaskets and vent membranes |
| Aluminum face | recessed perimeter land | die-cut VHB seal/bond, continuous pressure during cure |
| Chain | reinforced body-through hole | pull-tested boss, not a screw glued into a thin lid |

## Assembly order

1. Inspect the empty chassis.
2. Install vents and microphone gaskets.
3. Place the haptic motor in its pocket and strain-relieve its wires.
4. Place the approved battery in its cradle; route its leads without pinching.
5. Place and retain the PCB.
6. Fit the button plunger and silicone boot.
7. Run the electrical end-of-line test while the unit is still open.
8. Add the measured gap pad and close the face with the die-cut bond/seal.
9. Shake, listen, weigh, charge, record, reconnect and inspect every unit.

## The tests that prove it

- Shake/rattle recording before and after drops.
- Six-face, eight-corner and twelve-edge controlled drops using the supplied
  fixtures; open and inspect the first engineering units afterward.
- Chain pull, button life, haptic operation, charging and battery-temperature
  checks.
- Audio/BLE/backlog test before sealing and again after sealing.

CAD can show that the seats exist. Only built EVT units can prove compression,
adhesive cure, impact survival and silence. The included factory rules stop the
lot when one of these safety or function checks fails.
