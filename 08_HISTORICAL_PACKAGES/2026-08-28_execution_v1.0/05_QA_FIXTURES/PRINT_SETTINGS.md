# P2S print settings

Use PETG for the structural parts. PLA can crack at the collet fingers, bearing towers and adapter root. The cassette liners are TPU 95A.

## PETG

- 0.20 mm layer height
- 0.42 mm line width with a 0.4 mm nozzle
- 5 walls
- 6 top and 6 bottom layers
- 40% gyroid infill
- Dry filament before printing
- No “sport” or “ludicrous” mode
- Brim on the collet body, latch plate, bearing towers and edge adapter

Orient loaded parts so layer separation does not cross the load:

- Collet body, latch plate, rotor, cassette and pose keys: broad flat face on plate.
- Finger guides and fingers: largest flat face on plate.
- Bearing tower: print on its broad 55 x 78 mm side, then ream/check the bearing pocket.
- Edge adapter: upright as modeled with supports beneath the vertical plate; inspect every gusset layer.
- NEMA mount: broad plate on bed.

## TPU 95A

- 0.20 mm layers
- 3 walls
- 20% gyroid
- Slow external walls
- No support inside the liner cavity

## Mandatory calibration coupons

Before long prints, make simple coupons for:

1. 608 bearing pocket: target 22.2 mm nominal.
2. M4 heat-set insert bore: target 5.2 mm nominal, then use the insert maker’s actual recommendation.
3. Sliding finger: 10.0 mm finger inside 11.6 mm guide channel.

The finger must move under spring force without rocking enough to touch the DUT. Sand only the guide surfaces, never the release tip geometry.
