# P2S material and finish card

## What to use tonight

Use the **PLA Silk+ you already own**. It is good for checking the shape and
making a shiny visual prototype. It is not the material for a sellable pendant.

In Bambu Studio:

1. Select **Bambu Lab P2S**, 0.4 mm nozzle and the matching PLA Silk+ profile.
2. Use the supplied 3MF orientation; do not stand the shell on its end.
3. Use 0.12 mm layers, three walls and 25% gyroid infill.
4. Bambu's Silk/Silk+ guide recommends 235 °C nozzle temperature and the
   product guidance recommends slowing the outer wall to 50 mm/s for gloss.
5. Print one shell first. Dry-fit the plastic dummy parts. Only then print five.

Do not sand the first Silk+ shell. Sanding removes the shiny skin and makes the
surface patchy.

## If you want a better metal-looking plastic print

Order **Bambu PLA Metal — Iron Gray Metallic** from the Bambu Lab Canada
store. It was listed at CAD 31.99 when checked on 2026-08-29. It is still PLA
plastic; Bambu says it is designed to imitate metal texture. It does not become
an aluminum customer enclosure.

## The sellable material

- **Inside/back:** injection-molded Covestro Makrolon 2407 polycarbonate.
- **Front face:** 0.8 mm 5052-H32 aluminum, brushed and clear- or black-anodized.
- **RF end:** uncoated polycarbonate; metal cannot cover the Bluetooth antenna.

That stack gives the PLAUD-like cool metal face while keeping impact strength
and radio performance. A home FDM printer is for shape/fit prototypes, not the
finished metal consumer shell.

## If you want a painted show model

Print a second shell later in a normal or tough PLA, then use filler-primer,
wet-sand, metallic automotive paint and a compatible clear coat. Paint only an
empty shell in a ventilated area. Never spray a battery, microphone membrane,
button boot, charging contact or assembled electronics.
