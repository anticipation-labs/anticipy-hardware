# Print and metal card

## Unit 1

- Print the frame flat, back face on the build plate.
- Use a 0.20 mm layer height and a 0.40 mm nozzle.
- Use a tough nonconductive filament. Dry PETG is the emergency choice; PC/ABS is better only if the printer profile is already proven.
- Print the bridge, RF faces and all three plunger sizes.
- Do not scale any file.

## After unit 1 fits

- Print `anticipy_strict_evt_12_frame_plate.stl`.
- The plate deliberately contains 12 frames to target 10 passing builds.

## Aluminum

- Use `anticipy_strict_evt_aluminum_template.pdf` at exactly 100% scale or the front/back aluminum STEP files.
- Material: 0.60–0.65 mm 5052 aluminum.
- Cut and deburr one mirrored front/back pair first.
- Use a fine linear brush finish in the pendant's long direction.
- Keep every cut edge smooth; no burr may face the cell.
- Color-match the polymer RF nose with a nonconductive solid coating. Do not use metallic paint over it.

Finished nominal envelope: **56.0 × 23.0 × 12.0 mm**.
